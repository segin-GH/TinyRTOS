2 · Check one source

Use the vector-table and memory-model sections of the Arm Cortex-M3 Devices Generic User Guide. Read only enough to check your first prediction. Mark what changed in your explanation.

3 · Propose evidence

Your linker reserves 4 MiB of RAM. Which board or QEMU machine are you using, and what does its documentation say? If using Stellaris, consult QEMU's board inventory. Do not change the memory map until you identify the target.
