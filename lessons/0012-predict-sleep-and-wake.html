Who runs while tasks sleep?

Goal: predict one sleep-and-wake trace before checking it in the debugger. This connects your existing context switch to blocking, a foundation for later queues and synchronization.

Sleeping changes eligibility

Your scheduler now skips sleeping application tasks. A sleeping task still has its saved stack and execution position; it simply cannot be selected until the countdown makes it READY. Idle supplies an execution context when neither application task is ready. In your implementation, SysTick processes countdowns and requests PendSV; PendSV saves the outgoing context and selects the next eligible task.

Waking means becoming eligible, not restarting the task function. The selected task resumes its saved execution position, eventually returning from task_sleep and continuing its loop. Compare the blocked-task behavior in the primary reading: FreeRTOS vTaskDelay. Your API is your own implementation; use the source as a conceptual comparison.
