Vocabulary for an investigation

Prediction
An expected observation written before running an experiment.
Trace
A sequence of observed events or execution states.
Invariant
A condition your design must preserve at the points where it promises that condition holds.
Counterexample
A concrete case that contradicts a claim.

For architecture terms, consult the Arm guide after writing your prediction. We will grow the kernel glossary as concepts arise.
